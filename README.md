# kaam-sutra
This is just another To-Do application BUT with a twist.

We give some monetary value to each task.

> And remember, money is not just something that is issued by some goverment as a legal tender but anything where you put your faith and value in.

So, we give each task some weightage/value ( in various ways )

You need to earn as much money you can should to live happily. Just like you want to earn money. The more you earn, the better your life gets before reaching a point where the more time and effort you spend on money, the less life you live.

Whatever you want to do in your life, must be here. If it is important to you, give it corresponding value, if something you want to do but is not that important to you, add it here, but without time boundation.

> You work everyday and do the things you might or might not want to do and get paid for it, and then you can use that money to purchase things. But here, you work everyday and do what YOU want to do and get some money that you do not use to purchase items in your life, but is automatically redeemed as health, talent, love, happiness in your life whenever is needed. This money reduces the friction in your life. 

Things you can configure here:
- Add daily tasks that you want to be done without skipping. Like 10,000 steps a day, 1 hour guitar practise and so on. Do it and get the points, miss it, and it actually gets deducted from your salary ( Total points )
- Add weekly task with number of days you want it to be done like workout 4 times a week, 1 hour swimming once a week and so on.
- Add time bounded goals with decay value like learn handstand by the end of the year and get 100 points else with every delayed day, you get 1% less of the points.
- Add ubunbounded goals, that you just would want to happen but wouldn't care if didn't happen, like learn a kip-up. Whenever you learn it, you get the point. No decay.
- You can also add extra reward with diminishing percentage of returns for your surplus. Like get 10 more points for 2k more steps and 5 more for another 2k and so on. We dimish the points so that you don't try to over optimize only one thing just to find a loop hole and get more money.

> Behold, and remember, the money that you earn from here, is nothing but the Faith and hard work you have put in it, nothing something that you can use to purchase something in market. So don't try to cheat. Just like likes on your instagram post have no intrinsic value to itself, but a value you have put in it. If you give it too much improtance, every like becomes a commdotiy of value to you, and If you don't care about like, they stop praising and demotivate you from doing whatever you are doing. Put your faith in this currency, see your self worth and self love in it and it becomes as precious to you as real money. Earn more and keep it safe!
